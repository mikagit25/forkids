# ancient — I. Older Than History

Tuberculosis is caused by a slow-growing bacterium, Mycobacterium tuberculosis. It usually attacks the lungs, and spreads through the air when an infected person coughs. Genetic studies suggest that it emerged in Africa and spread around the world with human migration. The oldest confirmed human case comes from Atlit Yam, a submerged village off the coast of Israel, about nine thousand years old. Egyptian mummies show signs of tuberculosis of the spine. The ancient Greeks called it phthisis, the wasting disease. In English it became consumption, because it seemed to consume its victims [...]
