# today — IX. Not Gone

In wealthy countries, tuberculosis came to seem like a disease of the past. It was not. In 1993, the World Health Organization declared tuberculosis a global health emergency. HIV, which weakens the immune system, had fuelled a new wave of cases, and drug-resistant strains were spreading. Today, tuberculosis kills more than a million people every year, more than any other single infectious disease.
