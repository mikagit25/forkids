# closing — Epilogue

Tuberculosis has been with us for at least nine thousand years. We have understood it for less than a hundred and fifty, and been able to cure it for less than eighty. It is a reminder that a disease does not disappear just because we have the tools to stop it. It disappears only when those tools reach everyone.
