# koch — V. Koch's Discovery

For centuries, many doctors believed tuberculosis was hereditary, or caused by a weak constitution. On the twenty-fourth of March 1882, in Berlin, the German physician Robert Koch stood before the Physiological Society and announced that he had found its cause: a bacterium, which he had managed to stain and see under the microscope, and to grow in the laboratory. It was a turning point in medicine. Tuberculosis was infectious. It could be prevented. Today, the twenty-fourth of March is World Tuberculosis Day.
