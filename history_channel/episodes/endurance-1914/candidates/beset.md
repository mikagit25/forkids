# beset — III. Frozen In

After a last stop at the whaling stations of South Georgia, the Endurance sailed south on the fifth of December 1914. The Weddell Sea that year was choked with pack ice. For weeks the ship forced her way through narrow channels of open water. Then, in the middle of January 1915, barely a day's sail from the coast where Shackleton planned to land, the ice closed around her. The men tried to cut her free with picks, saws and chisels.
