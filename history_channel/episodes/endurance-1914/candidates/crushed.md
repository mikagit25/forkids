# crushed — V. The End of the Endurance

When spring came, the ice did not release the ship. It began to crush her. Huge pressure ridges pushed against the hull. The timbers groaned and cracked. Water poured in faster than the pumps could clear it. On the twenty-seventh of October 1915, in temperatures below minus twenty-six degrees, Shackleton gave the order to abandon ship. The men dragged their tents, stores and three lifeboats onto the ice. For almost a month the broken hull stayed upright, still held by the floe. There was one painful loss even before the ship sank.
