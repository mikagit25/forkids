# rescue — XI. Not a Life Lost

Shackleton immediately went back for the men on Elephant Island. Three times, the pack ice turned his rescue ships away. On the fourth attempt, he sailed aboard the Yelcho, a small steam tug of the Chilean Navy commanded by Luis Pardo. On the thirtieth of August 1916, after four and a half months on the island, the twenty-two men left behind saw the ship approaching. Frank Wild, Shackleton's second in command, had kept them going for four and a half months. They lived under the two upturned lifeboats, eating penguins and seals and burning blubber for warmth.
