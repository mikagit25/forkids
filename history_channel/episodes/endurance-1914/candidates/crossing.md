# crossing — X. Across the Mountains

The whaling station at Stromness lay on the other side of unmapped, glaciated mountains. No one had ever crossed them. Shackleton, Worsley and Crean set off with a rope, an axe and screws from the boat driven through their boots for grip. They walked for about thirty-six hours without sleep. At one point, lost on a ridge as night fell, they sat down together and slid down the mountainside on the coiled rope. On the twentieth of May 1916, three filthy, bearded men walked into Stromness. The station manager did not recognise them.
