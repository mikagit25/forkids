# found — Epilogue

For more than a century, the Endurance lay somewhere on the floor of the Weddell Sea. In March 2022, an expedition using underwater robots found her, three thousand and eight metres down, about four miles from the position Frank Worsley had recorded in 1915. She sits upright and remarkably well preserved. Her name can still be read across the stern. The wreck is now a protected monument. It will not be raised. And the story of the men who lost her is still told, in large part through the photographs Frank Hurley chose to save. If you enjoyed this story from the archives, subscribe for more.
