# drift — VII. Ocean Camp and Patience Camp

For five months the men lived on the drifting ice, first at a spot they called Ocean Camp, then at Patience Camp. They hunted seals and penguins for meat and blubber. When food ran short, the dogs had to be shot. Twice they tried to march towards land, dragging the lifeboats behind them. The snow was so soft that they covered barely a mile a day, and they gave up. Slowly, the ice carried them north, towards open sea. Every day the floe beneath them grew smaller and more fragile. On the ninth of April 1916, it split apart. Shackleton ordered the three boats into the water.
