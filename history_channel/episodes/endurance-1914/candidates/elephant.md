# elephant — VIII. Elephant Island

For seven days the boats fought through ice and freezing spray. Men were soaked, frostbitten and so thirsty they chewed raw seal meat for its moisture. On the fifteenth of April they landed on Elephant Island, a bare, storm-swept rock at the edge of the Antarctic Peninsula. It was the first time in four hundred and ninety-seven days that they had stood on solid ground. But nobody would ever look for them there. It was far from any shipping route. Shackleton decided that the only hope was to go for help himself, in a twenty-two-foot open boat, across the most dangerous ocean on Earth.
