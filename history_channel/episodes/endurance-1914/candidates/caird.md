# caird — IX. Eight Hundred Miles

The boat was called the James Caird. Shackleton chose five men to go with him, including the ship's captain, Frank Worsley, a navigator of genius, and the Irish sailor Tom Crean. Their destination was South Georgia, about eight hundred miles away. To miss it meant drifting into the open Atlantic and certain death. For sixteen days they sailed through gales and towering waves. Ice built up on the boat until it threatened to capsize her, and the men crawled out to chip it off. Worsley could take only four sightings of the sun in the entire journey, bracing himself on the pitching deck [...]
