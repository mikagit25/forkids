# winter — IV. The Long Night

The Antarctic winter brought months of darkness. The crew turned the ship into a home. They held football matches on the ice, raced their dog teams, staged concerts and shaved each other's heads for fun. Shackleton understood that boredom and despair could kill as surely as cold. He kept everyone busy and treated every man, from the officers to the youngest sailor, the same way. Among them was the expedition photographer, an Australian named Frank Hurley.
