# cold-open — 

In 1914, twenty-eight men sailed south to cross Antarctica on foot. They never set foot on the continent. Their ship was trapped in the ice, crushed, and dragged to the bottom of the sea. They lived for five months on floating ice, ate their dogs, and smashed hundreds of their own photographs to save weight. Six of them crossed eight hundred miles of the worst ocean on Earth in a lifeboat. Three of them walked over mountains no one had ever crossed.
