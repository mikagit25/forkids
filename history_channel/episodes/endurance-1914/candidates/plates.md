# plates — VI. What to Leave Behind

Every pound now mattered. Each man was allowed to keep two pounds of personal belongings. Hurley had already rescued his photographic plates from the flooded ship, diving into the icy water to reach them. Together with Shackleton, he sorted through around five hundred and fifty of them. He could carry only about a hundred and twenty. Hurley smashed the rest on the ice, so that he would never be tempted to go back for them. The images he kept are the ones you are seeing now.
