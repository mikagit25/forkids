# crew — II. The Crew

You may have heard of the newspaper advertisement Shackleton supposedly placed to find his crew: men wanted for a hazardous journey, small wages, bitter cold, safe return doubtful. It is a wonderful story. But historians have never found any trace of it in the newspapers of the time, and it was probably invented years later. What is true is that thousands of people applied. Shackleton chose his men in his own unusual way, sometimes after an interview lasting only a few minutes, asking whether a candidate could sing.
