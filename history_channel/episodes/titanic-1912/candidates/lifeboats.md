# lifeboats — 

There was one number that almost nobody thought about. The Titanic carried twenty lifeboats: fourteen large wooden boats, two smaller emergency cutters and four collapsible boats with canvas sides. Together, they could hold one thousand one hundred and seventy eight people. That was barely half of the people on board, and only about a third of the number the ship was allowed to carry. It was entirely legal. The British rules on lifeboats had been written in 1894, for ships of ten thousand tons and more. Nobody had imagined a ship of more than forty six thousand.
