# iceberg — 

Up in the crow's nest, twenty nine metres above the deck, the lookouts Frederick Fleet and Reginald Lee were peering into the dark. They had no binoculars. A pair had been left behind in Southampton, and their requests for another had come to nothing. There was no moon, and the sea was as flat as glass. Without waves, there was no white water breaking at the foot of an iceberg to give it away. At twenty one minutes to midnight, Fleet saw a dark shape ahead.
