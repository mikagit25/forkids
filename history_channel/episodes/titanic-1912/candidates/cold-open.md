# cold-open — 

At twenty to midnight on Sunday the fourteenth of April 1912, a lookout high above the deck of the largest ship in the world rang the bell three times and picked up the telephone. Iceberg, right ahead, he said. Less than three hours later, the ship was gone. Of the two thousand two hundred people on board, around fifteen hundred died in the freezing water of the North Atlantic. The ship was the Titanic. Her name has become a byword for disaster.
