# sinking — Chapter 5

By two o'clock in the morning, all the regular lifeboats had gone. The bow was under water. Some fifteen hundred people were still on board. On deck, the ship's band, led by the violinist Wallace Hartley from Colne in Lancashire, kept playing to keep people calm. Legend says the last piece was the hymn Nearer, My God, to Thee. Some survivors remembered only cheerful tunes. None of the eight musicians survived.
