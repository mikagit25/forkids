# boats — Chapter 4

Shortly after midnight, Captain Smith ordered the lifeboats uncovered and the passengers woken. Then came the order: put the women and children in and lower away. At first, few people wanted to leave a huge, warm, brightly lit ship for a small open boat on a black sea. At a quarter to one, the first boat, number seven, was lowered with only twenty eight people on board. It could hold sixty five. The two officers in charge read the order differently.
