# departure — Chapter 2

The maiden voyage began on Wednesday the tenth of April 1912, at Southampton. Special trains brought passengers from London. As the Titanic left her berth, the suction from her huge propellers pulled a smaller liner, the New York, away from her moorings. Only a quick order from Captain Smith and a tugboat avoided a collision. It was a bad omen, people said afterwards. That evening, the Titanic called at Cherbourg in France.
