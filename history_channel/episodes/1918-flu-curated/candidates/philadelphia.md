# philadelphia — IV. The Parade

Nowhere shows the cost of a bad decision more clearly than Philadelphia. By late September, sailors at the city's navy yard were already falling ill. Many local doctors urged the authorities to cancel public events. But on the twenty-eighth of September, the city went ahead with a huge parade to sell war bonds. Officials had expected around ten thousand spectators. More than two hundred thousand people packed the streets, shoulder to shoulder, cheering the marching bands.
