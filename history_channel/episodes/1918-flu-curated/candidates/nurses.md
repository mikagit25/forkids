# nurses — V. The Nurses

If doctors could do little, nurses could do a great deal: keeping patients warm, fed and hydrated could be the difference between life and death. But there were not enough of them. More than half of the army's twenty thousand nurses had already sailed to Europe when the second wave hit America. The American Red Cross launched a national appeal. It placed newspaper advertisements, asked hospitals to release staff, and accepted nurses still in training.
