# name — II. The Wrong Name

The world would come to call it the Spanish flu. But it almost certainly did not start in Spain. Spain was neutral in the war, and its newspapers were not censored. While the warring nations kept news of sickness quiet to protect morale, Spanish papers reported the epidemic openly, including the illness of their own king, Alfonso the Thirteenth. So it looked, to the rest of the world, as if Spain was the worst hit. In Spain, meanwhile, many people called it the French flu. Censorship did more than give the disease the wrong name.
