# second-wave — III. The Second Wave

In late August 1918, the virus came back, and it had changed. Almost at the same time, a far more lethal form appeared in ports on three continents: Brest in France, Freetown in Sierra Leone, and Boston in the United States. Outside Boston, at Camp Devens, the army hospital filled within days. In September, the army sent one of the country's most respected doctors, Victor Vaughan, to see it for himself. He found every bed taken and young men dying in the corridors. On the day he arrived, sixty-three soldiers died. Healthy people could feel well at breakfast and be dead by nightfall. [...]
