# brevig — X. The Village in the Ice

In November 1918, the virus reached Brevig Mission, a tiny village on the coast of Alaska. In five days, seventy-two of its eighty adults died. They were buried together in a mass grave in the frozen ground. In 1951, a young Swedish scientist named Johan Hultin dug into that grave, hoping to recover the virus. He failed. Forty-six years later, at the age of seventy-two, he went back. This time he found the body of a woman, preserved by the permafrost, whose lungs still carried fragments of the virus's genetic code.
