# kansas — I. A Cook in Kansas

The story is usually told starting at Camp Funston, a vast army training camp in Kansas. On a morning in early March 1918, a company cook reported to the infirmary with a fever, a sore throat and aching muscles. By lunchtime, dozens more soldiers had joined him. Within weeks, more than a thousand men were sick. Most of them recovered. This first wave looked like an ordinary, if unusually fast, spring flu, and newspapers barely noticed.
