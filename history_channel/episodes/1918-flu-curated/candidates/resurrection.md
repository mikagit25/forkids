# resurrection — XI. Bringing It Back

Working with Jeffery Taubenberger's team, who had pieced together the virus's genes from preserved tissue samples, scientists finally had the complete genetic recipe of the 1918 virus. In 2005, at a high-security laboratory at the US Centers for Disease Control, the microbiologist Terrence Tumpey reconstructed it. The precautions were extraordinary. Only Tumpey was allowed to handle the virus. He worked alone, after hours, passing fingerprint and iris scans to enter the lab, and took an antiviral drug every day in case of infection.
