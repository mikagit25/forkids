# masks — VII. Masks and Rebels

Doctors in 1918 had no vaccine against influenza, and no antibiotics to treat the bacterial pneumonia that killed so many patients. They did not even know that the disease was caused by a virus. That would not be discovered until the 1930s. So cities fought back with the only tools they had: closing schools, churches and theatres, banning public gatherings, and ordering people to wear gauze masks.
