# samoa — VIII. Two Islands

Perhaps the starkest lesson of all came from two neighbouring groups of islands in the South Pacific. On the seventh of November 1918, a steamer called the Talune arrived at Apia, in Western Samoa, then governed by New Zealand. Sick passengers were allowed ashore. Two weeks later, a New Zealand newspaper reported that Samoa had cabled for help, and that none could be spared from New Zealand. The government was trying to find doctors in Australia instead.
