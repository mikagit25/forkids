# cold-open — 

For thousands of years, one disease killed rich and poor, kings and children, on every inhabited continent. It killed roughly three in every ten people it infected, and left many survivors scarred or blind. People fought it with prayers, with red cloth, and with a dangerous trick learned from Asia and Africa: deliberately infecting the healthy. Then an English country doctor tried something stranger still, using a disease of cows. And in 1980, for the first and only time in history, the world declared a human disease eradicated. This is the biography of smallpox.
