# killer — II. A Killer of Kings

Smallpox shaped history. When Europeans arrived in the Americas, they brought smallpox with them. Indigenous peoples had never encountered it, and epidemics devastated the Aztec and Inca empires, helping a few hundred Spanish soldiers conquer them. In 1520, smallpox swept through the Aztec capital, Tenochtitlan, and killed the new emperor, Cuitláhuac, after only eighty days on the throne.
