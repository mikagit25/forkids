# balmis — VI. A Living Chain

There was a problem. Without refrigeration, the vaccine could not survive long sea voyages. In 1803, the Spanish Crown found an extraordinary solution. A doctor named Francisco Javier de Balmis sailed for the Americas with a group of orphan boys. Before departure, two boys were vaccinated. As the sores developed, material from them was used to vaccinate the next two, and so on, keeping the vaccine alive, child to child, across the Atlantic.
