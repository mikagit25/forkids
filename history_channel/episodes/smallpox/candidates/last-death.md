# last-death — IX. The Last Victim

There was one more tragedy. In 1978, a medical photographer named Janet Parker, who worked at Birmingham University Medical School in England, caught smallpox from a laboratory in the building where the virus was being studied. She died, the last person known to have been killed by smallpox. The head of the laboratory, the virologist Henry Bedson, blamed himself. While in quarantine at home, he took his own life.
