# today — Epilogue

The virus still exists, but only in two high-security laboratories, one at the Centers for Disease Control in the United States and one at the VECTOR institute in Russia. Whether to destroy these last stocks is still debated. Strangely, the vaccine that finally defeated smallpox was not cowpox at all. After a century and a half of being passed from arm to arm and grown on the skin of calves, it had become a different virus, called vaccinia.
