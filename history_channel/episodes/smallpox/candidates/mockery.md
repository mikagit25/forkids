# mockery — V. Cows and Cartoons

Not everyone was convinced. Some clergymen objected to infecting humans with animal disease. Critics warned that vaccinated people might start to look like cows. In 1802, the caricaturist James Gillray drew a famous cartoon showing Jenner vaccinating a crowd of frightened patients, with tiny cows bursting out of their arms and faces. But vaccination was far safer than variolation, and it spread rapidly. Jenner's work was translated into several languages within a few years.
