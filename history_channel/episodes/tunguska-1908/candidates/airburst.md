# airburst — VIII. An Explosion in the Air

Today, most scientists agree on what happened. A space rock, most likely a stony asteroid around fifty or sixty metres across, entered the atmosphere at enormous speed. The pressure and heat became too much for it. Several kilometres above the ground, it exploded. That is why there was no crater. The object never hit the ground. Its energy was released in the air, as a blast wave and a flash of heat. The upright trees at the centre had been directly underneath.
