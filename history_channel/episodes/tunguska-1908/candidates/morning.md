# morning — I. Thirtieth of June

The date was the thirtieth of June 1908, by the modern calendar. In the Russian Empire, which still used the old Julian calendar, it was the seventeenth. Shortly after seven in the morning local time, people scattered across the vast forests of central Siberia saw a column of blue-white light move across the sky, almost as bright as the sun. Then came a flash, and a sound like artillery fire, rolling on and on.
