# world — III. Bright Nights

The rest of the world did not know where the explosion had happened. But it noticed. Seismic stations as far away as Irkutsk, Tashkent, Tiflis and Jena recorded the shock as if it were a moderate earthquake. Barographs across the world, including in London, registered the pressure wave as it travelled around the planet. It was recorded in Berlin and Potsdam, in Zagreb, and as far away as Batavia, in the Dutch East Indies, and Washington.
