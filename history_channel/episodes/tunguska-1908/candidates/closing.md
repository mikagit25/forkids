# closing — Epilogue

Today, the thirtieth of June is marked around the world as Asteroid Day, chosen in memory of Tunguska. It was founded in 2014 by a group that included the physicist Stephen Hawking and the astrophysicist and Queen guitarist Brian May, and in 2016 the United Nations proclaimed it an international day. Space agencies now track thousands of near-Earth objects. On the twenty-sixth of September 2022, NASA's DART mission deliberately crashed a spacecraft into a small asteroid moon called Dimorphos.
