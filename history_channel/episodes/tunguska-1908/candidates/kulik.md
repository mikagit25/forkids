# kulik — IV. The Meteorite Hunter

The Russian Empire had other concerns. Then came the First World War, the revolution and the civil war. It was not until 1921 that a scientist went looking. His name was Leonid Kulik, a mineralogist who specialised in meteorites. Kulik had lived an eventful life. Born in 1883 in what is now Estonia, he had served in the Russo-Japanese War, spent time in jail for revolutionary activity, and fought again in the First World War, before becoming a teacher of mineralogy and then a researcher at the Mineralogical Museum in Petrograd.
