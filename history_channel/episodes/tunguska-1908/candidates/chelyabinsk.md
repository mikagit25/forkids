# chelyabinsk — IX. It Happened Again

For a long time, Tunguska seemed like a once-in-a-millennium event. Then, on the fifteenth of February 2013, a much smaller asteroid, about eighteen metres across, exploded over the Russian city of Chelyabinsk. This time thousands of cameras were watching. The blast released around half a megaton of energy. The flash was brighter than the sun. Minutes later, the shock wave arrived. It shattered windows across the city, and one thousand four hundred and ninety-one people sought medical help, almost all of them hurt by flying glass.
