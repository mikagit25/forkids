# theories — VII. A Hundred Theories

A mystery with no crater attracted every kind of explanation. Over the decades, people proposed a comet, a chunk of antimatter, a tiny black hole, a natural gas explosion from beneath the ground, and, inevitably, a crashed alien spaceship. Most of these ideas fall apart under the evidence. The serious debate was between two candidates. In 1934, the British meteorologist Francis Whipple suggested a small comet, a ball of ice and dust that would vaporise completely in the air and leave nothing behind.
