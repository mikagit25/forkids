# haiti — III. Napoleon's Lost Army

In 1802, Napoleon sent a large army to the Caribbean colony of Saint-Domingue, today's Haiti, to crush the revolution of formerly enslaved people and restore French control. The army fought the Haitian revolutionaries. But it was also attacked by yellow fever. By one account, around fifteen thousand French soldiers died of fever in only two months. Tens of thousands died in all, from war and disease, among them the expedition's commander, Napoleon's brother-in-law, General Leclerc.
