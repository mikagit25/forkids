# today — Epilogue

Yellow fever has not been defeated. It still circulates in parts of Africa and South America, and causes tens of thousands of deaths in some years, mostly where vaccination coverage is low. Around six hundred million people live in areas where the disease is found. The World Health Organization estimates around two hundred thousand cases a year, about nine in ten of them in Africa. In 2017, an outbreak in Brazil moved toward the crowded Atlantic coast, where most people had not been vaccinated.
