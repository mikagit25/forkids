# camp — VI. Proof

To be certain, the commission built an experimental station near Havana, named Camp Lazear in honour of their friend, and ran a careful series of experiments. In one building, volunteers slept for weeks among the soiled bedding and clothing of yellow fever patients. None of them caught the disease. In another, clean and well-ventilated, volunteers were exposed only to infected mosquitoes. They fell ill.
