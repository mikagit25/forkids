# gorgas — VII. War on the Mosquito

If mosquitoes were the problem, mosquitoes could be fought. In Havana, the army doctor William Gorgas launched a campaign against them in 1901. Gorgas knew the disease personally. Years earlier, at an army post in Texas, he had caught yellow fever while caring for a patient, a young woman named Marie Doughty. Both survived, immune for life, and later they married. Water containers were covered, breeding sites destroyed, and houses fumigated by burning pyrethrum powder. Within about a year, the number of cases in Havana fell from hundreds to none.
