# reed — V. The Reed Commission

In 1900, after the Spanish-American War, yellow fever was killing American soldiers in Cuba. The US Army sent a commission led by Major Walter Reed to find out why. The commission decided to test Finlay's mosquito theory, on human volunteers. On the thirty-first of August 1900, Dr Jesse Lazear allowed a mosquito that had fed on yellow fever patients to bite a soldier, Private William Dean. Within days, Dean fell ill. He survived.
