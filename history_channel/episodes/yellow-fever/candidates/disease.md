# disease — I. Yellow Jack

Yellow fever is caused by a virus, carried by mosquitoes, above all the species Aedes aegypti. It begins like many fevers, with chills, headache and muscle pain. Most patients recover. But about fifteen in every hundred go on to a severe form of the disease. But in some, after a brief remission, the disease returns violently. The liver fails, turning the skin and eyes yellow. Patients bleed from the gums and stomach, and vomit dark, digested blood, the dreaded black vomit. The virus and its mosquito originally came from Africa.
