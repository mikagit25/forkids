# cold-open — 

In 1793, it drove the government of the United States out of its own capital. A few years later, it destroyed one of Napoleon's armies in the Caribbean. It killed thousands of French workers trying to dig a canal across Panama. For more than a century, doctors argued about how it spread: through filth, through bedding, through the air. The answer turned out to be a mosquito. Proving it cost the lives of brave volunteers. And that discovery made the Panama Canal possible. This is the biography of yellow fever.
