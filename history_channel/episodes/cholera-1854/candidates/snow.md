# snow — II. Dr Snow

John Snow was born in York in 1813, the first of nine children. His father was a labourer in a coal yard, in one of the poorest parts of the city. At fourteen, Snow was apprenticed to a surgeon near Newcastle, and in 1832, still a teenager, he met cholera for the first time, treating the sick in the coal-mining village of Killingworth. He became a vegetarian and a teetotaller, and he drank only water that had been boiled. He worked his way up to become one of the leading doctors in London.
