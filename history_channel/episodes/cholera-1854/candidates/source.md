# source — VII. The Cesspit

Henry Whitehead, the clergyman who helped Snow, had at first believed in the miasma theory himself. Snow's map changed his mind. Whitehead kept digging, and he found what was likely the starting point. At a house on Broad Street, a baby had fallen ill with cholera just before the outbreak. Her mother had washed the baby's nappies, and poured the water into a cesspit at the front of the house. That cesspit lay only about three feet from the well, and its brickwork was leaking. The water people trusted most had been mixing with sewage all along.
