# outbreak — III. Broad Street

The outbreak in Soho began on the thirty-first of August 1854. In the first three days, a hundred and twenty-seven people on or near Broad Street died. Within a week, three quarters of the residents had fled. By the tenth of September, more than three hundred were dead. Snow later called it the most terrible outbreak of cholera which ever occurred in this kingdom. Many of the sick were carried to the Middlesex Hospital.
