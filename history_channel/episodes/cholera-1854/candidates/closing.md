# closing — Epilogue

Today, John Snow is considered one of the founders of epidemiology, the science of tracking how diseases spread. His method is still taught to every new generation of public health students: find the cases, map them, and pay attention to the exceptions. Cholera has not gone away. It still infects an estimated three to five million people a year and kills tens of thousands, almost always where people lack clean water and sanitation.
