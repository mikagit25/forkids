# experiment — VI. The Grand Experiment

Snow had a second piece of evidence, and it was even more powerful. South London was supplied by two private water companies, whose pipes ran down the very same streets. Neighbours on the same road, living the same lives, could be customers of different companies without even knowing it. One company, the Lambeth Company, had moved its intake upriver a few years earlier, away from the sewage of central London.
