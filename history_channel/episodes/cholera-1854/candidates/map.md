# map — IV. The Map

With the help of a local clergyman, the Reverend Henry Whitehead, Snow gathered the name and address of every victim he could find. He marked each death on a map of the area as a small black bar. The bars piled up around one spot: the Broad Street pump. Then he looked at the exceptions, because exceptions are where the truth hides. A brewery stood right next to the pump. None of its workers caught cholera. They were given a daily allowance of beer, and drank that instead of water from the well.
