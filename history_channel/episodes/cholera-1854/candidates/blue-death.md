# blue-death — I. The Blue Death

Cholera came from India. In 1817 it began to spread out of Bengal along trade routes and with armies, and over the following years it swept across Asia, Russia and Europe. It arrived in Britain for the first time in October 1831, in the port of Sunderland, carried by a ship from the Baltic. It came back again and again, killing tens of thousands. In London alone, the epidemics of 1832 and 1849 killed more than fourteen thousand people. It was a horrifying disease. Doctors described the diarrhoea as rice water: an untreated patient could lose ten to twenty litres of fluid in a day. [...]
