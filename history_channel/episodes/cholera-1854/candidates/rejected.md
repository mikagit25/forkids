# rejected — VIII. Ignored

You might expect the authorities to have celebrated Snow. They did not. London's leading medical officer, John Simon, called Snow's theory peculiar. The official Board of Health report put the outbreak down to bad air. The Board's own scientists even saw tiny organisms, which they called vibriones, in water samples, but none of them suspected that these could be the cause. Many experts found the idea that people were drinking each other's waste simply too disgusting to accept. Another clue was missed entirely.
