# handle — V. The Pump Handle

On the evening of the seventh of September, Snow presented his evidence to the local parish authorities, the Board of Guardians. They were sceptical. But they agreed to act. The next day, the pump was put out of use. According to the famous story, its handle was removed. The outbreak was probably already fading by then, as people fled and the source weakened. Snow himself admitted it. But the investigation had done something more important than stopping one outbreak. It showed, with names, addresses and a map, where the disease was coming from.
