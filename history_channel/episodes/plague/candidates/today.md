# today — X. Plague Today

Today, the plague is treatable. Antibiotics such as streptomycin cure most patients if they are treated early. But the bacterium still lives in wild rodents on several continents, including the western United States. Every year there are human cases, most of them in Africa, especially Madagascar. In 2017, an outbreak of pneumonic plague in Madagascar caused more than two thousand cases and around two hundred deaths before it was brought under control.
