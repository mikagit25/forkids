# aftermath — VII. A Changed World

The Black Death transformed European society. With so many workers dead, survivors could demand higher wages and better conditions. Landowners and governments tried to freeze wages by law. In England, King Edward the Third issued the Ordinance of Labourers in 1349, fixing wages at their level before the plague, and Parliament confirmed it with the Statute of Labourers in 1351. Landlords who needed tenants began to accept rents in money instead of labour in their fields.
