# yersin — IX. Hong Kong, 1894

At the end of the nineteenth century, a third great plague pandemic began in China. In 1894 it reached Hong Kong. The first case was found on the eighth of May, a young hospital worker, and two days later the colony was declared an infected port. Two scientists raced to find its cause. One was the Japanese bacteriologist Kitasato Shibasaburō, sent by his government, who arrived on the twelfth of June with a team of five, and was given access to the bodies at the city's hospital.
