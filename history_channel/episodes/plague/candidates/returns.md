# returns — VIII. The Plague Returns

The plague never really left. It came back to Europe every few years for nearly four centuries. It began quietly. On the thirtieth of April 1665, Samuel Pepys noted in his diary the first cases in the city. The Great Plague of London went on to kill around a hundred thousand people, almost a quarter of the city, in eighteen months. Weekly bills of mortality listed the dead parish by parish; they recorded sixty-eight thousand plague deaths, and the real number was certainly higher.
