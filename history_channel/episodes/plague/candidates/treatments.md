# treatments — VI. Bleeding and Theriac

What could doctors actually do? Very little. They let blood, to rebalance the body's humours. They lanced buboes. They prescribed theriac, an ancient compound medicine with dozens of ingredients, sometimes including viper flesh. The wealthy were advised to flee early, go far, and return late. The most famous image of the plague doctor, with a long beaked mask stuffed with herbs, a waxed coat and a stick, does not actually come from the Black Death.
