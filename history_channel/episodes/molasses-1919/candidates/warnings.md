# warnings — III. The Leaking Tank

The tank had been built in 1915, in a hurry. The man in charge of its construction was the company's treasurer, Arthur Jell. He had no training as an engineer or architect. The tank was never properly tested with water to check for leaks before the molasses went in. From its very first filling, it leaked. Brown streaks ran down its sides. The company's response was to paint the tank brown, so the leaks would not show. Local families came with buckets and cans to collect the molasses that oozed out.
