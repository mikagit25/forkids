# noon — IV. 12:30 p.m.

The fifteenth of January 1919 was unusually mild. The day before, it had been bitterly cold. Now the temperature rose to around forty degrees Fahrenheit. At about half past twelve, as dock workers ate lunch and children played outside, people nearby heard a deep rumble, followed by sharp cracks, like a machine gun. Others said it felt like the ground was shaking, or that it sounded like a passing elevated train. Rivets were shooting out of the tank's steel plates. Then the tank split open.
