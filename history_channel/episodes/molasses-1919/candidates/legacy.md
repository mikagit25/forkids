# legacy — Epilogue

The case is considered a milestone in the development of modern corporate regulation. The verdict changed the rules. Massachusetts, and then states across the country, began requiring that large structures be designed and approved by licensed engineers and architects, with their calculations on file. The tank was never rebuilt. The site became a yard for the elevated railway, and today it is a city park, Langone Park, with a baseball field, a playground and bocce courts.
