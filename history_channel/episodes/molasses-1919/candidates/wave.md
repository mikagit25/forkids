# wave — V. The Wave

The wave smashed through everything in its path. Steel panels from the tank hurtled into the girders of the elevated railway, nearly bringing the tracks down. A streetcar was pushed off its rails. Buildings were knocked from their foundations. A truck was picked up and thrown into the harbour. The Boston Globe reported that people were picked up by a rush of air and hurled many feet. People and horses were caught in the flood. Molasses is heavy and thick. Once it engulfed someone, they could barely move or breathe.
