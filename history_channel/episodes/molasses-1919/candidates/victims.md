# victims — VI. The Victims

The dead came from every part of the neighbourhood's life. Six of them worked at the city's paving yard beside the tank: labourers, a paver and two blacksmiths. There were teamsters and drivers with their wagons, a longshoreman, an expressman and a messenger. George Layhe, a firefighter of Engine 31, died when the wave smashed into his firehouse. In a house nearby, sixty-five-year-old Bridget Clougherty was killed when her home was flattened, and Stephen Clougherty, thirty-four, died too. The oldest victim, Michael Sinnott, was seventy-eight.
