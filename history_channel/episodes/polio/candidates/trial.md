# trial — X. The Polio Pioneers

In 1954, Salk's vaccine was put to the test in the largest medical experiment the world had ever seen. It was designed and led by Thomas Francis, Salk's former teacher, at the University of Michigan, and paid for by the March of Dimes. One point eight million American children took part. They were called the Polio Pioneers. About four hundred and forty thousand received the vaccine, about two hundred and ten thousand received a harmless placebo, and more than a million were simply observed, to see who caught polio.
