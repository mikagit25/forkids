# fdr — IV. The President in the Wheelchair

In August 1921, a thirty-nine-year-old lawyer and rising politician went on holiday with his family to Campobello, a Canadian island off the coast of Maine. His name was Franklin Delano Roosevelt. During the trip he fell into the cold waters of the Bay of Fundy. A few days later he had a high fever, and then he could no longer move his legs. Doctors diagnosed polio. Some modern researchers think it may have been a different illness, Guillain–Barré syndrome. Either way, Roosevelt was left paralysed from the waist down for the rest of his life.
