# lung — VI. The Iron Lung

Polio's most frightening form attacked the muscles used for breathing. Patients slowly suffocated. In 1928, at Harvard, Philip Drinker and Louis Agassiz Shaw built a machine to breathe for them. The first iron lung was tested on the twelfth of October 1928 at the Children's Hospital in Boston. It was powered by an electric motor and two vacuum cleaners. The patient lay inside a sealed metal tank with only the head outside.
