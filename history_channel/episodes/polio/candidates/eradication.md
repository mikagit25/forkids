# eradication — XIV. The Last Two Countries

In 1988, the World Health Assembly voted to eradicate polio from the planet, just as smallpox had been. The Global Polio Eradication Initiative, led by the World Health Organization with Rotary International, UNICEF, the CDC and later the Gates Foundation, became the largest international public health effort in history. That year, polio paralysed an estimated three hundred and fifty thousand people in a hundred and twenty-five countries. Vaccinators went door to door, village to village, with drops of oral vaccine.
