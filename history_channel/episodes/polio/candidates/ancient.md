# ancient — I. An Old Disease

Polio is caused by a virus that lives in the human gut. It spreads mostly through contaminated food and water, by what doctors call the faecal–oral route. About three quarters of infections cause no symptoms at all. Some people get a sore throat and a fever, and recover. But in a small proportion of cases, the virus attacks the nerve cells of the spinal cord that control the muscles, and the result is paralysis. The disease is very old.
