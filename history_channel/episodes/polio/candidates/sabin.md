# sabin — XII. Sugar Cubes

Salk's rival, Albert Sabin, believed there was a better way: a vaccine made from live but weakened virus, swallowed instead of injected. It would protect not only the person who took it, but also stop the virus spreading in the community. Sabin's vaccine needed a huge trial, and in the middle of the Cold War, he found it in the Soviet Union. From 1956, he worked with Soviet scientists, who organised the first mass production.
