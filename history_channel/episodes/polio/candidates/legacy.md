# legacy — XIII. What Polio Left Behind

Polio left behind millions of survivors. The World Health Organization estimated in 2001 that there were between ten and twenty million of them around the world. Many grew up in a world that was not built for them. There were no ramps, and few buses or schools that a wheelchair could enter. Children were sent to separate institutions, or carried up and down stairs. As they grew older, polio survivors began to demand equal rights.
