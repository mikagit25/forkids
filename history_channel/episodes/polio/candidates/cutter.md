# cutter — XI. The Cutter Incident

The triumph was followed almost immediately by a disaster. Several companies had been licensed to make the vaccine, and had already produced large stocks. At one of them, Cutter Laboratories in California, some batches contained live poliovirus that had not been properly killed, even though they had passed the required safety tests. About a hundred and twenty thousand doses of this vaccine were given to children. Forty thousand developed a mild form of polio. Fifty-six were paralysed, and five died. The virus spread to their families and neighbours, paralysing a hundred and thirteen more [...]
