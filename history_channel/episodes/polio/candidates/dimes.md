# dimes — V. A Dime for Every Child

In 1938, Roosevelt helped found the National Foundation for Infantile Paralysis. Its fundraising campaign got a name from the entertainer Eddie Cantor, as a pun on a popular newsreel called The March of Time. He called it the March of Dimes, and asked Americans to send a dime, ten cents, straight to the White House. At first only a few dimes arrived. Then came the flood. Within days, the White House mail room was receiving forty to fifty thousand letters a day, so many that the President's official mail was buried under them.
