# cold-open — 

Every summer, for half a century, parents in America and Europe were afraid. Swimming pools closed. Cinemas stood empty. Children were kept indoors, away from fountains and beaches and other children. A disease came with the warm weather, and nobody knew how to stop it. Most of the people it infected never noticed a thing. But some woke up unable to move their legs. Some could not breathe, and spent months, years, or the rest of their lives inside a metal machine that breathed for them.
