# copenhagen — VII. Copenhagen, 1952

In the summer of 1952, polio hit Copenhagen. The city's hospital for infectious diseases had just one iron lung and a few other breathing devices. Within weeks it was overwhelmed with paralysed patients, many of them young children. Nine out of ten patients with the worst form of the disease were dying. An anaesthetist, Bjørn Ibsen, noticed something. The patients were not only failing to breathe. Their swallowing muscles were paralysed too, and they were drowning in their own saliva.
