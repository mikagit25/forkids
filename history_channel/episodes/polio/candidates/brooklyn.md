# brooklyn — III. The Summer of 1916

On Saturday the seventeenth of June 1916, the health authorities of New York announced an epidemic of infantile paralysis in Brooklyn. Most of the first cases were in the old South Brooklyn district, in Italian immigrant neighbourhoods. Within ten days there were more than two hundred cases. It became the worst polio epidemic the world had yet seen. That year, the United States recorded more than twenty-seven thousand cases and over seven thousand deaths.
