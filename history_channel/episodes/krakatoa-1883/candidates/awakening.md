# awakening — II. The Awakening

On the twentieth of May 1883, Perboewatan woke up. Steam and ash rose about six kilometres into the sky. The explosions could be heard in Batavia, the colonial capital, a hundred and sixty kilometres away, where they rattled windows and doors. The colonial authorities were curious rather than afraid. On the twenty-sixth of May, an excursion party was sent out to the island to have a look. They found the island covered in fine white dust, and watched Perboewatan explode every five to ten minutes, throwing out pumice. Then the eruption seemed to calm down.
