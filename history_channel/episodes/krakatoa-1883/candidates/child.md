# child — XI. The Child of Krakatoa

For more than forty years, the sea over the old volcano was quiet. Then, in early 1927, signs of volcanic activity appeared in the middle of the caldera, between where the cones of Perboewatan and Danan had once stood. On the twenty-ninth of December 1927, a new island broke the surface. Waves washed it away. It appeared again, and was washed away again. Finally, on the eleventh of August 1930, it rose permanently above the sea. It was named Anak Krakatau, the child of Krakatoa.
