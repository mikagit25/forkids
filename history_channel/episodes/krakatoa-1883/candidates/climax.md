# climax — III. Sunday, the Twenty-Sixth

On Sunday the twenty-sixth of August, at about one o'clock in the afternoon, the eruption entered its final, violent phase. By two o'clock, a black cloud of ash stood twenty-seven kilometres high. The explosions came every ten minutes, then almost without stopping. By five in the afternoon they could be heard all over Java. Ships in the strait were caught in the darkness. Within twenty kilometres of the volcano, pieces of hot pumice as big as a fist rained down on their decks.
