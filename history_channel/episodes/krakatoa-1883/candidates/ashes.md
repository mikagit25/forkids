# ashes — VI. The Burning Ashes

There was another killer. Clouds of incredibly hot gas, ash and pumice, called pyroclastic flows, poured down the sides of the volcano and raced across the sea on a cushion of steam. They travelled forty kilometres across the strait and reached the coast of Sumatra, where they burned villages and forests. Around noon, a rain of hot ash fell on the district of Ketimbang. It became known as the Burning Ashes of Ketimbang. About a thousand people died. One survivor, Mrs Beyerinck of Ketimbang, later described what happened in her house. Suddenly, it became pitch dark.
