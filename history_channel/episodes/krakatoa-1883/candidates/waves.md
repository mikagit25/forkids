# waves — V. The Waves

The explosions themselves killed few people. The sea killed tens of thousands. As huge masses of hot ash and rock fell back into the strait, they pushed the water outwards in a series of giant waves. The largest followed the third explosion. At Merak, on the north-western tip of Java, the wave was estimated at forty-six metres high, as tall as a fifteen-storey building. The town was destroyed. Along the west coast of Java the waves reached more than forty metres, and along the south coast of Sumatra more than twenty.
