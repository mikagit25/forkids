# explosions — IV. The Loudest Sound

On Monday the twenty-seventh of August came the great explosions. The first great explosion came at about half past five in the morning. The second followed at a quarter to seven. The third, at two minutes past ten, was the biggest of all. It was heard three thousand one hundred kilometres away in Perth, in Western Australia. On the island of Rodrigues, near Mauritius, nearly five thousand kilometres away, people thought it was cannon fire from a ship nearby. The sound had taken about four hours to travel there.
