## The channel
Classical Night Relax published long-form classical music programs (1–8 hours) for sleep, relaxation and study,
for an adult audience (not "made for kids"). The music consisted of historical classical works (Chopin, Bach,
Beethoven, Mozart, Debussy, Tchaikovsky and others) in recordings obtained from Musopen (musopen.org), a non-profit
library that publishes public-domain and openly licensed recordings. The visuals were our own: still images with
slow camera movement. Each video description listed the composers and works.

## History relevant to the decision
- In September 2026 the channel received one copyright strike for a recording of Wagner's "Tannhäuser". The
  recording had been offered as public domain, but a record label holds rights in it. After the strike we removed
  all recordings from that source from every program, marked them as not to be used, and did not upload them again.
- The channel was previously used for another topic and was renamed when its theme changed to classical music.
- We did not receive any other strike, warning or notice before the termination.

## Why the decision appears disproportionate
- A single copyright strike, already addressed, does not lead to termination under YouTube's own three-strike
  policy, and the termination notice refers to the Terms of Service in general rather than to copyright.
- No statement of reasons identifying the content or the rule was given, which makes it impossible to correct
  any problem or to respond to it.
- Termination of the whole Google account is the most severe measure available; a less restrictive measure
  (removal of specific videos, a warning, a temporary restriction) would have been sufficient for any issue
  related to individual videos.
- I am willing to remove any video the platform considers problematic and to follow any instruction about the
  sources of recordings.
